# Operations-Maintenance-Process-Automation-and-Tracking
## **Project Overview**

Maintenance operations often involve multiple activities that must happen in sequence: scheduling maintenance, collecting information, notifying relevant teams, tracking execution, following up on outstanding actions, monitoring performance, and preparing periodic reports.

When these activities rely heavily on manual communication and disconnected spreadsheets, several operational challenges can arise:

- Delayed or inconsistent notifications
- Incomplete or inconsistent data capture
- Difficulty tracking outstanding maintenance actions
- Limited visibility into maintenance status
- Repetitive manual reporting
- Difficulty identifying overdue activities
- Limited management visibility into maintenance performance
- Increased risk of human error during data entry and reporting

This project involves the design of an end-to-end Maintenance Operations Process Automation System that connects these activities into a structured workflow.

**The solution combines:**

- Digital Maintenance Notification
- Centralized Maintenance Tracking
- Maintenance Performance Dashboard
- Automated Maintenance Reporting

I redesigned a maintenance operations workflow to reduce manual coordination, standardize data capture, automate notifications, centralize maintenance tracking, and improve management visibility through automated reporting and dashboards

## Business Problem

The maintenance workflow requires coordination between maintenance planning, field execution, issue tracking, follow-up, and reporting.

A purely manual process can make it difficult to answer important operational questions quickly:

- What maintenance activities are scheduled?
- Which sites require maintenance?
- Who is responsible for each activity?
- Which activities have been completed?
- Which actions are still pending?
- Which maintenance activities are overdue?
- How many issues remain unresolved?
- What types of maintenance issues occur most frequently?
- How is maintenance performance changing over time?
- What information should be included in the weekly management report?

The system was therefore designed around a central principle:

Capture operational data once, use it throughout the workflow, and automate repetitive downstream activities wherever possible.

## Project Objectives

The project was designed to achieve the following objectives:

  1. **Standardize maintenance data capture:** create a structured method for collecting maintenance information rather than relying on inconsistent manual communication.
  2. **Improve notification workflow:** automate maintenance notifications so that relevant information is communicated consistently.
  3. **Centralize maintenance records:** create a structured tracker that provides a single operational view of maintenance activities and outstanding actions.
  4. **Improve monitoring and accountability:** track maintenance status, assigned personnel, planned activities, completed activities, pending actions, and overdue items.
  **5. Improve management visibility:** transform maintenance records into an interactive dashboard containing operational KPIs and visual analysis.
  **6. Reduce repetitive reporting work:** create a reporting workflow that makes weekly maintenance reporting more structured and efficient.
  **7. Support data-driven decision-making:** Use operational data to identify trends, recurring issues, performance gaps, and areas requiring follow-up.
